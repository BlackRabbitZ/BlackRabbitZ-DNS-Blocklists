# Licensing and provenance policy

The repository's code, original documentation and original curated dataset are published under GPL-3.0-only, subject to the notices in `NOTICE` and `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## No list laundering

Do not copy another maintainer's list into `data/` and remove its attribution. A domain name is a fact, but collections can carry copyright, database, contractual or attribution obligations depending on jurisdiction and source.

Contributors must submit independently verified domains or material they have the right to contribute.

## External sources

No third-party blocklist is imported by default. If external data is ever added, its license must be reviewed first and its provenance/license recorded in `THIRD_PARTY.md`. Incompatible or unclear sources must not be merged.

This policy is designed to reduce licensing risk, not to provide a legal guarantee.
