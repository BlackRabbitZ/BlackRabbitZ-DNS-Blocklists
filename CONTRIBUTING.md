# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve BlackRabbitZ DNS Blocklists.

## Domain submissions

Every addition needs:
1. the exact domain,
2. a category,
3. independent evidence,
4. a short explanation of expected blocking impact.

Do not paste bulk content from another blocklist.

By contributing, you certify that you have the right to submit the contribution under GPL-3.0-only and that any factual domain observations were independently verified or lawfully sourced.
